How to Connect a Canon PIXMA Printer to WiFi
============================================

Connecting a Canon PIXMA printer to WiFi lets you print from compatible computers, phones, and tablets without keeping a USB cable attached. First, connect the printer to your wireless router. Then add it to the devices you want to print from.

The exact Canon PIXMA wireless setup process depends on your model. Some printers have a touchscreen for choosing the network, while others use an application or a model-specific button sequence.

Before You Begin
----------------

Check the model name printed on your printer. Not every PIXMA model includes WiFi, so confirm that yours offers wireless networking before starting.

Prepare the following:

* Your WiFi network name and password.
* A computer or phone connected to your home network.
* A powered-on printer with initial setup completed.
* Paper loaded for a test print.
* Compatible installation software if required.

Place the printer within a reliable signal range of the router. Resolve any error displayed on the printer before configuring its connection.

Check Your Wireless Network
---------------------------

Your printer needs a network that matches its wireless capabilities. Many PIXMA models use 2.4 GHz WiFi, while some also work with 5 GHz.

Check your exact model's specifications rather than assuming that every PIXMA printer uses the same wireless bands.

The printer and computer can use different bands if they are on the same local network and the router allows communication between them.

Avoid guest networks that isolate connected devices. Enter the current WiFi password exactly, including capital letters and special characters.

Connect a Canon PIXMA Printer Using Its Screen
----------------------------------------------

If your printer has a display with network settings, you may be able to configure WiFi directly from its control panel.

Open Wireless Setup
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Find the network settings or wireless LAN setup menu. Menu names vary between models.

Choose the option for connecting through a wireless router. This is different from connecting a device directly to the printer.

Select Your Network
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Choose your WiFi network from the available list.

If the network does not appear, move the printer closer to the router and search again. A hidden network may require manual entry where that feature is available.

Enter Your Password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Type the WiFi password carefully and confirm the connection.

Wait until the printer reports that setup is complete. If available, check its network information to confirm that it joined the intended network.

Canon PIXMA WiFi Setup Without a Screen
---------------------------------------

For a printer without a display, use the setup application or button procedure intended for your exact model.

Open a compatible installation application and choose wireless setup. Follow its instructions to place the printer into connection mode.

Select your network and enter the password when prompted.

During setup, your phone or computer may temporarily connect directly to the printer. Continue until configuration finishes, then confirm that your device returns to your home network.

Do not copy button sequences from a different PIXMA model. Controls and indicator patterns vary.

Connect a Canon PIXMA Printer Through WPS
-----------------------------------------

WPS can establish a wireless connection without manually entering the network password when both the printer and router offer the feature.

Start the printer's WPS setup process using the instructions for your model. Activate the router's WPS function within the required connection window.

Wait for the printer to confirm the connection.

Check the router's button label carefully. Do not press its reset button instead of WPS, because resetting can erase network settings.

If WPS is unavailable or unsuccessful, use the screen-based or application-assisted method.

Add Your Wireless Printer to Windows
-------------------------------------

After connecting the printer to WiFi, add it to your computer.

On Windows 11, open Settings, select Bluetooth & devices, and choose Printers & scanners.

On Windows 10, open Settings, select Devices, and choose Printers & scanners.

Choose the option to add a printer and wait for discovery. Select your Canon PIXMA model and complete the prompts.

If additional software is required, choose a trusted installation package intended for the exact model and operating system.

Open a short document, select the newly added printer, and print one page.

Connect a Canon PIXMA Printer to a Mac
--------------------------------------

Connect the Mac to the same local network as the printer.

Open the Mac's printer settings and choose the option to add a printer. Select your PIXMA model from the available devices.

Choose AirPrint when available for your configuration, or select compatible printer software.

Complete installation and open a document. Check the selected printer, paper size, and orientation before printing.

Print from a Phone or Tablet
---------------------------

Use a compatible mobile printing application or an available built-in printing feature.

Connect the phone or tablet to the same local network as the printer. Open the document or photograph, choose Print, and select your PIXMA model.

If the printer does not appear, check its wireless connection and whether your network allows devices to discover one another.

Available printing options depend on your printer, mobile operating system, and application.

Canon PIXMA Wireless Setup Without a CD
---------------------------------------

A CD is not always necessary to connect a Canon PIXMA printer to WiFi.

Compatible devices may add the printer through their built-in printer settings. Other configurations require a separate setup application or driver package.

Verify the model, operating system, and software publisher before installing anything. Avoid unrelated driver updater applications.

Reconnect After Changing Your Router
------------------------------------

A new router, WiFi name, or password can make the printer's saved network details outdated.

Run wireless setup again and enter the current details.

If the printer connects but the computer cannot print, check whether its existing printer entry refers to the previous network configuration.

Adding the printer again may refresh that entry. Reset network settings only when necessary, because doing so removes saved wireless information.

Canon PIXMA Printer Not Connecting to WiFi
------------------------------------------

Check the Network Name
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Make sure you selected your intended network rather than a nearby network with a similar name.

Check the Password
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Enter the current WiFi password. This may differ from the password used to manage your router.

Check Wireless Compatibility
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Confirm that the router provides a wireless band and security configuration compatible with your model.

Do not leave your network open as a workaround.

Improve Signal Strength
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Move the printer closer to the router while testing. Thick walls, enclosed cabinets, and interference can weaken the connection.

Restart and Retry
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Restart the printer and the device performing setup.

If other devices also have network problems, restart the router and allow it to finish starting before trying again.

Printer Connected to WiFi but Not Printing
------------------------------------------

A successful wireless connection does not automatically confirm that every computer has the correct printer configuration.

Check the printer selected in your application's print dialog. An old USB entry or duplicate installation may still be selected.

Open the print queue and remove failed jobs. Resume printing if paused.

Try printing from another device. If that works, focus on the affected computer's queue, printer entry, and software.

WiFi Through a Router and Wireless Direct
-----------------------------------------

A router connection places the printer on your home network so compatible devices can communicate with it.

Wireless Direct, where available, connects a device directly to the printer. It does not join the printer to your router through the same process.

Choose the method that matches your intended use. Connecting through the router is convenient when several home devices need to print.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

Can every Canon PIXMA printer connect to WiFi?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

No. Connection options vary by model. Check that your printer includes wireless networking.

Do I need a USB cable for wireless setup?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Many models allow wireless setup without a USB cable. Follow the installation method available for your exact printer.

Can my printer and laptop use different WiFi bands?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Yes, provided both are on the same local network and the router allows communication between them.

Do I need to reconnect every time I print?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Normally, the printer saves its network settings. Reconnection may be needed after a router change, password change, or network settings reset.

How do I confirm the connection works?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Add the printer to your device and print a short document. Check that the job completes and the expected page prints.

Complete Your First Wireless Print
----------------------------------

Select your Canon PIXMA printer in the print dialog, review the paper settings, and send one page.

Once the test succeeds, continue with your usual documents. If your model includes scanning, test that function separately using a compatible scanning application.
